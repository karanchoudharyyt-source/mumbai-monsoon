# Prompt

Paste this into [Droid](https://factory.ai) to build your own version.

```
Build a browser game as one self-contained HTML file using three.js from a CDN. Generate every texture, sign and sound in code: no image, model or audio files.

Setting: driving an auto-rickshaw down a Mumbai street at midnight in heavy monsoon rain.

- City: a long road with sidewalks and cross streets, tall buildings with lit windows and coloured curtains, shopfronts with rolling shutters, glowing shop signs in English and Hindi (chai, vada pav, Irani cafe, medical store, tailors), black water tanks and billboards on the rooftops, sagging overhead cables, street lamps with warm halos, yellow-and-black kerbs.
- Wet road: real mirror reflections of every light and sign, rain ripples, splashes, bloom and fog.
- Rain: thousands of streaks lit by headlights and lamps, plus lightning that flashes the whole sky, with thunder.
- The auto: yellow and black, canvas hood, "बुरी नज़र वाले तेरा मुँह काला" painted on the back, an MH number plate, a driver in khaki. Traffic of other autos, black-and-yellow taxis, cars, red buses, trucks and bikes that keep their lanes and brake.
- HUD: a speedometer and a red digital fare meter that starts at ₹26.
- Cameras: a chase cam, a cockpit view with working wipers and drops on the windscreen, and a low cinematic cam. Add an autopilot mode.
- Sound with the Web Audio API: rain, an engine that follows your speed, and a horn.
- Controls: keyboard on desktop, on-screen touch buttons on phones.

Then render screenshots from every camera, look at them critically, and fix anything that looks fake or broken. Repeat until every shot looks like a film still.
```

Short version (fits in one post):

```
One HTML file, three.js, no image/model/audio files. A night drive in a Mumbai auto in monsoon rain: wet mirror road, neon signs in English + Hindi, traffic, lightning, wipers, ₹26 fare meter, chase + cockpit cams, Web Audio sound, touch controls.
```
